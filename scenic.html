Famous attractions in Shandong

Mount Tai

The foremost of the Five Great Mountains, it is a site of both World Cultural and Natural Heritage. It has been a place for emperors throughout the ages to perform sacrificial rituals. The mountain's majestic grandeur and its sunrise scenery are renowned worldwide.

The Three Memorial Edifices of Confucius in Qufu

The Confucian Temple, Confucius Mansion, and Confucius Cemetery, the birthplace of Confucian culture, are ancient architectural complexes commemorating Confucius

Bao Tu Spring

The foremost among Jinan's 72 Springs, it is a landmark of the Spring City, with springs gushing year-round and a profound historical heritage.

Qingdao Pier

Qingdao, a symbol of the city, boasts beautiful coastal scenery and is a popular destination for watching the sea and feeding seagulls.
