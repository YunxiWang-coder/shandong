Shan Dong Travel Guide

The best time to travel

April to May and September to October offer pleasant weather, ideal for hiking, exploring the Spring City, and enjoying seaside activities; summer is suitable for coastal cities like Qingdao and Weihai.

Travel Route Reference

Classic Route: Jinan (Baotu Spring, Daming Lake) → Tai'an (Mount Tai) → Qufu (Three Confucian Sites) → Qingdao (coastal scenery).

Travel Tips

1. When climbing Mount Tai, it is recommended to bring comfortable sports shoes, and you can check the weather forecast in advance if you plan to watch the sunrise;
2. Coastal cities are abundant in seafood; when enjoying seafood, pay attention to food hygiene;
3. Shandong has a rich culture of etiquette, and visitors should observe the sites with civility.

Transportation Suggestions

The province has a well-developed high-speed rail network, with Jinan and Qingdao serving as transportation hubs, making travel between cities very convenient.
